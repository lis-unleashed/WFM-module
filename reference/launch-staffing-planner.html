How this is worked out

Calls, chats and emails are active customers × contacts per customer each month × the launch uplift. Claims use their own rate with no uplift, split into urgent and routine. Every volume is spread across open days by your weekday shares, then across each day by its own pattern, and moves with the low and high range.

Every workstream is sized with Erlang C against its own target. Chat time is divided by the chats handled at once. Routine claims count each lodgement, chase and completion as a separate piece of work. An urgent claim is handled start to finish in one go.

Because everyone is cross-skilled, the team works the most urgent item first. For each 15 minutes, the planner checks each service level in order of how quickly work must be started. It sizes the team for that work plus everything more urgent, then takes the largest answer, never below minimum cover and never above the occupancy cap. This is a standard planning approximation, and real results are worth checking against actuals once you're live.
